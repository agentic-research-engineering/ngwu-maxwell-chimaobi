# Research Project Operating Instructions

This repository supports the undergraduate Philosophy project **A Philosophical Investigation into the Institutions of State in Francis Fukuyama's The Origins of Political Order**. The human researcher remains the author. Assist with research, analysis, drafting, revision, and audit; never manufacture scholarship.

## Read before work

1. Read `research-bible/00-project-charter.md`, `03-table-of-contents.md`, `05-source-hierarchy.md`, and `06-citation-and-evidence-rules.md`.
2. Read the research map, claims, gaps, and decisions relevant to the requested section.
3. Load the relevant repository skill under `skills/`.

The table of contents in `research-bible/03-table-of-contents.md` is authoritative. Do not silently change it or overwrite the manuscript or source files. Put new working research in the Research Bible or a clearly named research note.

## Evidence and method

- Treat Fukuyama's 2011 book as the principal object; use the 2014 companion as continuation, not replacement.
- Authority order: primary texts; peer-reviewed/scholarly sources; institutional sources; scholarly reference works; general/tertiary sources.
- Label research claims as `[FUKUYAMA]`, `[PRIMARY-OTHER]`, `[SCHOLAR]`, `[INTERPRETATION]`, `[INFERENCE]`, `[CONTESTED]`, `[UNVERIFIED]`, or `[RESEARCHER-THESIS]`.
- Verify exact quotations, editions, locations, bibliographic data, and paraphrases. Never guess. Use `[CITATION NEEDED]` or `[UNVERIFIED]` when proof is absent.
- Keep exposition, reconstruction, comparison, analysis, and evaluation distinct. Historical material serves a philosophical inquiry.
- Distinguish state, state capacity, rule of law, accountability, democracy, government, political order, development, and decay.
- Record major claims in `09-claims-register.md`, quotations in `15-quotation-ledger.md`, sources in `16-bibliography-register.md`, gaps in `10-research-gaps.md`, and settled choices in `19-research-decisions.md`.

## Workflow

Define the section question; check existing materials and registers; consult primary texts; consult relevant scholarship; use web research only when repository evidence is insufficient and cite authoritative sources; record provenance, disagreement, and uncertainty; update registers; then synthesize or draft. For conflicting evidence, preserve each position, compare source authority and scope, and explain the resolution or leave the issue contested.

Do not draft chapter prose unless requested. When drafting is requested, preserve researcher authorship and the approved structure. Audit completed work for structure, concepts, argument validity, source-to-claim fit, quotations, citations, and bibliography correspondence before calling it complete.

## Source cautions

The Bookey PDF and the seven-page derivative article are tertiary aids, not evidence of Fukuyama's claims. The full 667-page 2011 PDF is the current canonical research copy, subject to edition/pagination verification. See `16-bibliography-register.md`.
