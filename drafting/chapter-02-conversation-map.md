## Delete-the-Author-Names Pre-Drafting Test

Each planned movement remains intelligible without proper names: macrohistorical scale; state-capacity differentiation; legal autonomy; accountability thresholds; specialist qualification of civilizational comparison; probabilistic evolution; convergence and plural paths; integrative gap. The planned architecture is therefore debate-led rather than author-led.
