# Footnote Normalization Audit

## Governing convention

The working departmental precedent establishes true footnotes, full first citation, shortened nonconsecutive citation, and `Ibid.` only for immediately consecutive unambiguous citations. Because chapters may circulate independently and the precedent does not establish thesis-wide carryover, first-citation form resets at each chapter.

- author names and ordering;
- book and journal title treatment;
- article quotation marks;

- place, publisher, and year;

- shortened titles and unambiguous `Ibid.`;

- note-to-claim attachment;
- one-to-one matching of Markdown references and definitions.

## Locator policy

Fukuyama notes use chapter and, where useful, named-section anchors. Classical sources use stable chapter, section, preface, or part locators. Specialist works retain verified article ranges or adopted relevant page spans. No converted-PDF page is represented as a print page.
