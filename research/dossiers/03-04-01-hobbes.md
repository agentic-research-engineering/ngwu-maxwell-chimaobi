## Phase 2 Primary Findings

`[HOBBES-PRIMARY]` In *Leviathan* ch. 13, equality of vulnerability and hope produces competition, diffidence, and glory under the absence of a common power; “war” names a known disposition to conflict, not uninterrupted fighting. Chs. 14–15 derive laws of nature directing peace, covenant keeping, and other social virtues. Ch. 17 explains the commonwealth as authorization of one representative power able to secure peace and defence. Hobbes therefore does not claim humans lack reason, norms, local affections, or every cooperative capacity before the state. `[FUKUYAMA-ON-HOBBES]` Fukuyama is right that Hobbes makes coercive common power necessary for secure order, but his contrast with natural sociability risks overstating Hobbes's atomism. Secondary check: SEP notes current scholarship rejects a simple psychological-egoist reading and treats the moral status of Hobbes's laws of nature as disputed. Final citations must be reconciled to the Tuck 1996 edition.
