## P0 Closure Findings

Contemporary anthropology strongly supports Fukuyama's empirical rejection of originally isolated humans. Forager evidence supports flexible band residence but corrects any picture of closed kin groups; “tribe” is a contested heuristic, not a mandatory evolutionary stage. Religion and warfare are qualified mechanisms, not necessary or sufficient master causes. This empirical result does not by itself invalidate Hobbes's structural model, Locke's juridical condition, or Rousseau's conjectural genealogy.
