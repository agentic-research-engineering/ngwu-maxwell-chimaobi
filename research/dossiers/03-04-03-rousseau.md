## Phase 2 Primary Findings

`[ROUSSEAU-PRIMARY]` The *Discourse* explicitly presents conjectures rather than a demonstrated empirical history. Natural humans are relatively solitary, self-preserving, capable of pity, and neither locked in Hobbesian war nor yet equipped with developed moral relations. Dependence, comparison, property, and social institutions transform psychology and inequality. The political compact within the *Discourse* may entrench the rich rather than provide a fully legitimate state. `[FUKUYAMA-ON-ROUSSEAU]` Fukuyama accurately sees a less conflictual natural condition but risks making Rousseau's methodological abstraction answer the same empirical question as evolutionary anthropology. “Noble savage” should be treated as shorthand, not Rousseau's precise doctrine. Final quotations must be checked against the Cress 1994 translation.
