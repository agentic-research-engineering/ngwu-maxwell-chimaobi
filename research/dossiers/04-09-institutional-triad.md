## Phase 2 Findings

`[FUKUYAMA]` The state concentrates and uses power; rule of law subjects that power to public, transparent rules; accountability subordinates rulers to the governed (ch. 1, PDF pp. 30–31). The three emerge separately, as his contrasts among Afghanistan, Russia, and Singapore illustrate. `[INTERPRETATION]` The relationship is one of functional complementarity and tension, not synonymy or automatic co-development.
