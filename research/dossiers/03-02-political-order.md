## Phase 2 Findings

`[FUKUYAMA]` Modern political order is not democracy alone. Fukuyama separates state, rule of law, and accountable government, then defines liberal-democratic success as their stable balance (ch. 1, canonical PDF p. 30; print page unavailable). `[INTERPRETATION]` The triad combines functional analysis with a normative reference point: capacity enables collective action, while law and accountability constrain and direct power. Hewson's academic review supports this reconstruction but notes the work is thematic and selective rather than a general political history.
