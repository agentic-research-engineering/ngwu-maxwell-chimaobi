### India
STATUS: **RESOLVED AS PLAUSIBLE BUT UNDERDETERMINED.**

Specialist work supports substantial but uneven Mauryan centralization, historically variable Brahmanical influence, and religious endowments with fiscal/jurisdictional autonomy. It does not establish religion or caste as the primary cause of weaker centralization. State and religious authority were reciprocally constitutive; rival mechanisms remain.

### Christianity and Kinship
STATUS: **RESOLVED BY QUALIFICATION.**

Distinctive Church family rules and an association with weaker intensive kinship are supported. Uneven enforcement, persistent/reconfigured kinship, regional variation, and observational causal inference leave the extended sequence to individualism and impersonal political institutions contested.

### Evolutionary Anthropology
STATUS: **RESOLVED WITH REVISION.**

Human sociality and contributions from kin selection and reciprocity are strongly supported. Current scholarship requires multiple genetic, cultural, reputational, normative, and cooperative-breeding mechanisms. Forager bands were flexible and substantially nonkin; tribe/chiefdom/state categories cannot be used as a universal ladder. Religion and warfare are qualified mechanisms.

## Major Findings

- The triad distinguishes capacity, legal constraint, and responsiveness in a philosophically useful way.
- The dimensions can arise separately and remain in permanent tension; “balance” is not frictionless equilibrium.
- Strict historical determinism is not supported by Fukuyama's texts.
- Early Chinese centralization is well supported; calling it modern is a restricted administrative stipulation.
- India and Western Europe demonstrate historically plural relations among state, social organization, religion, and law.
- Contemporary anthropology supports natural human sociality but not a universal linear ladder of political forms.
- Empirical anthropology can test historical assumptions without automatically defeating the philosophical functions of state-of-nature arguments.

## Major Qualifications

- Responsiveness is broader than accountability in actor–forum/sanction theories.
- War, religion, kinship weakening, and military slavery are neither necessary nor sufficient single causes of state formation.
- Brahmanical authority and royal power were mutually shaping, not simply opposed.
- Church marriage rules likely weakened or reorganized some intensive kin structures; they did not erase kinship or independently cause modern impersonality.
- Kin networks can enable administration as well as produce patrimonial capture.
- Convergence on functional solutions is an argument requiring defense, not an observed universal law.

## Rejected Initial Assumptions

- Fukuyama's theory is strictly historically deterministic.
- Every society follows one necessary institutional sequence.
- China was comprehensively modern in the liberal/constitutional sense.
- India lacked meaningful centralized states.
- Church policy simply destroyed European kinship.
- Kin selection and reciprocal altruism alone explain human large-scale cooperation.
- Anthropological inaccuracy by itself invalidates Hobbes, Locke, or Rousseau.
- The departmental sample is scholarly authority for substantive claims.

## Contested Questions

- The exact causal weight of religion/caste in Indian political divergence.
- The long path from Church family rules to impersonal political institutions.
- Qin/Han bureaucratic maturity and the comparative meaning of “modern.”
- The appropriate conceptual threshold for accountability.
- Whether Fukuyama's convergence benchmark is a useful ideal type or teleological residue.
- Whether particular instances of repatrimonialization are decay or adaptation.

These are understood disagreements with evidence and bounded formulations, not unresearched gaps.

## Emerging Central Thesis

`[RESEARCHER-THESIS][PROVISIONAL]` Fukuyama's triad is most defensible as a comparative diagnostic framework for distinguishing state capacity, legal constraint, and political responsiveness, not as a universal evolutionary sequence. Its philosophical value lies in exposing permanent tensions among these institutions; its limit appears when broad functional categories—modern state, accountability, weakened kinship, and convergence—compress historically plural mechanisms and convert qualified comparisons into a common endpoint.

The human researcher must approve or revise this thesis before it governs prose.
