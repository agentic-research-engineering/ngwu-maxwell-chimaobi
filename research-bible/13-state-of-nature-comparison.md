## Phase 2 verification note

- **Hobbes:** Chapters 13–17 confirm equality, insecurity, laws of nature, covenant, and authorization. War is a condition/disposition under absent common power, not constant combat. Simple asocial egoism is not established.
- **Locke:** §§4–21, 25–51, 95, 123–131 confirm freedom/equality under natural law, the nature/war distinction, prepolitical property, consent, and government's remedial purpose.
- **Rousseau:** Preface and Parts I–II confirm an explicitly conjectural method, relative solitude/pity, and the transformation produced by dependence/property. “Noble savage” remains an interpretive label.
- **Comparative result:** Fukuyama's empirical-evolutionary question overlaps with but does not fully replace the normative and conjectural purposes of the earlier models.
