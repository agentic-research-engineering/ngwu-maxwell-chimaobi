# Research Infrastructure Bootstrap Report

## 1. Repository Inspected

The repository contained five files: one student-authored DOCX and four PDFs. The DOCX contains the project title page, approved table of contents, abstract, and bibliography; extracted text showed no substantive chapter drafts. The PDFs comprise complete copies of Fukuyama's 2011 and 2014 books, a Bookey chapter summary of the 2011 book, and a seven-page Wikipedia-derived overview. The original files were not modified.

## 2. Primary Sources Identified

- Francis Fukuyama, *The Origins of Political Order* (2011): complete 667-PDF-page copy; canonical project research copy pending print-page/edition verification.
- Francis Fukuyama, *Political Order and Political Decay* (2014): complete 674-PDF-page companion copy.

The manuscript bibliography names other primary philosophers, but their full texts are not in the repository.

## 3. Secondary Materials Identified

No standalone peer-reviewed secondary scholarship is stored in the repository. The manuscript bibliography lists relevant classic and comparative works but their entries and use remain to be verified. The Bookey and Wikipedia-derived PDFs are tertiary materials only.

## 4. Research Bible Created

Files `00` through `20` establish the charter, questions, argument map, authoritative TOC, concepts, source/evidence rules, method/style, claims, gaps, Fukuyama and comparative maps, literature/quotation/bibliography registers, chapter maps, open questions, decisions, and this audit report.

## 5. Skills Created

Twelve skills cover source research, Fukuyama close reading, philosophical and comparative analysis, literature review, citation verification, argument audit, chapter research/writing, source synthesis, fact-checking, and final thesis audit.

## 6. Thesis Architecture

Chapter One is supported by the charter, question, concept, method, and style files. Chapter Two is supported by the literature matrix and source workflow. Chapter Three uses the anthropology/argument and state-of-nature maps. Chapter Four uses the institutional, civilizational, decay, and cross-volume maps. Chapter Five uses the evaluation criteria, claims register, objections workflow, and audit skills. Every approved numbered section has a research-map row.

## 7. Source Quality Findings

- The three *Origins*-named PDFs are not equivalent: one is the full text; one is Bookey; one is a short derivative overview.
- Ebook conversion means PDF page numbers may not equal published print pages.
- The existing bibliography includes incomplete entries (notably Hegel and Aristotle) and many unverified publication details.
- No direct scholarly literature on Fukuyama was found locally.
- No independent specialist histories were found locally for the China, India, Islamic, and European claims.

## 8. Research Gaps

P0 gaps: confirm citation style and controlling edition; obtain direct Fukuyama scholarship; verify state-of-nature comparisons from original philosophers; test civilizational histories with specialist scholarship; extract exact triad passages; source the strongest criticisms; audit all bibliography entries. P1 gaps include chapter-one evidence packets, cross-volume concept mapping, exact rule-of-law/accountability definitions, scholarly reception of strengths, and modern-history scope.

## 9. Risks

Major risks are insufficient secondary scholarship, unstable page citations, overdependence on Fukuyama for his historical evidence, conceptual slippage within the triad, premature determinism/Eurocentrism critiques, an unverified bibliography, and contemporary claims becoming a scope diversion.

## 10. Recommended Research Sequence

1. Confirm departmental citation rules and select a citation-stable edition.
2. Close-read and ledger the core definitional passages in *Origins* chapters 1, 2, 5, 17, 22, 28–30.
3. Build the direct Fukuyama reception literature cluster.
4. Verify Hobbes, Locke, and Rousseau from primary texts and scholarship.
5. Research each civilizational case with specialist sources, starting with claims used to establish the triad.
6. Map the 2014 continuation only after the 2011 framework is secure.
7. Develop Chapter Five objections after exposition and historical verification.
