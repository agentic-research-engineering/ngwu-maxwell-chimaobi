# Project Charter

## Project

**Title:** A Philosophical Investigation into the Institutions of State in Francis Fukuyama's *The Origins of Political Order*

**Discipline:** Philosophy, using conceptual analysis, textual interpretation, argument reconstruction, comparison, critical evaluation, and historically informed evidence.

## Objective

Investigate how Fukuyama explains the emergence, differentiation, interaction, and decay of the state, rule of law, and political accountability, and evaluate the philosophical adequacy of that framework.

## Boundaries

- The 2011 *Origins* is the principal text; the 2014 *Political Order and Political Decay* is a companion.
- The approved table of contents is fixed unless the researcher explicitly changes it.
- Bootstrap and research maps are not thesis prose.
- Existing manuscript and source files are preserved.
- The researcher owns all interpretive and evaluative conclusions.

## Required distinctions

Every phase must distinguish exposition (what the author says), reconstruction (how claims connect), comparison, analysis, and evaluation. Evidence labels and verification rules in files 05 and 06 are mandatory.

## Definition of done

A section is research-ready only when its question, claims, evidence, counterarguments, gaps, and source locations are recorded. A draft is complete only after argument, citation, quotation, structure, and bibliography audits.
