# Literature Review
## Purpose
Map debates and locate the project's warranted contribution.
## When to Use
For Chapter Two or updating the literature matrix.
## Required Inputs
Research question, thematic cluster, verified scholarly sources.
## Sources to Read First
Research Bible 01, 05, 10, 14, 16; relevant primary text for the debate.
## Procedure
Define conversation; select authoritative/relevant scholarship; record question, argument, method, evidence, relation to Fukuyama, agreement/disagreement, limits; synthesize positions and unresolved issues; identify a gap without claiming novelty prematurely.
## Evidence Rules
Read enough of each source to represent its argument; abstracts support discovery, not detailed attribution.
## Output Format
Thematic synthesis plus updated matrix rows and gap statement.
## Quality Gates
Sources converse around questions; disagreements and methods are explicit; coverage is balanced.
## Failure Conditions
Only general summaries, isolated quotations, or bibliography metadata are available.
## Prohibited Behaviors
Annotated-bibliography prose, artificial consensus, irrelevant source stuffing.
## Completion Criteria
The review explains the field, the unresolved problem, and this project's bounded role.
