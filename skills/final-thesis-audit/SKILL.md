# Final Thesis Audit
## Purpose
Determine whether the thesis is submission-ready without rewriting its authorship.
## When to Use
After all chapters are drafted and revised.
## Required Inputs
Complete manuscript, authoritative TOC, all Research Bible registers, required style guide.
## Sources to Read First
Research Bible 00–19; all cited source copies; department requirements.
## Procedure
Check TOC/heading coverage; central-question alignment; concept consistency; exposition/evaluation separation; argument validity; every major claim against register; every quotation/paraphrase; in-text/footnote–bibliography correspondence; duplicate/edition consistency; cross-chapter contradictions; unresolved markers; formatting requirements. Classify findings by severity and re-audit repairs.
## Evidence Rules
Sample checks are insufficient for quotations, bibliography correspondence, or unresolved placeholders: inspect all.
## Output Format
Gate-by-gate PASS/PASS WITH WARNINGS/FAIL; finding ID, location, severity, repair; final readiness verdict.
## Quality Gates
No invented or unverified citation; no missing approved section; no high-severity argument/evidence fault.
## Failure Conditions
Source files unavailable, manuscript incomplete, or institutional rules unknown for final compliance.
## Prohibited Behaviors
Declaring pass based on file existence, concealing uncertainty, or silently changing thesis claims.
## Completion Criteria
All mandatory gates pass and remaining warnings are explicitly acceptable to the researcher.
