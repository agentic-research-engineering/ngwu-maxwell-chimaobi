# Fact Checking
## Purpose
Test non-interpretive claims against appropriate authoritative evidence.
## When to Use
For dates, offices, events, statistics, publication data, and civilizational history.
## Required Inputs
Atomic claim, time/place scope, current citation.
## Sources to Read First
Research Bible 05, 06, 09, 10, 16; relevant primary or institutional records.
## Procedure
Decompose compound claim; identify best authority; verify date/definition/geography and source currency; triangulate consequential or contested facts; record result, conflicts, and correction; update registers.
## Evidence Rules
Contemporary claims need current sources; secondary macrohistory needs specialist corroboration.
## Output Format
Claim; verdict; evidence; conflict/qualification; corrected wording; citation status.
## Quality Gates
Authority is competent and current for the fact; exact scope matches wording.
## Failure Conditions
Only circular citations, summaries, or inaccessible evidence exist.
## Prohibited Behaviors
Using plausibility as verification or silently choosing among conflicting figures.
## Completion Criteria
Claim is verified, narrowed, contested, or marked unverified.
