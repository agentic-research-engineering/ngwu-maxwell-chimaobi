# Chapter Writing
## Purpose
Draft only from a completed evidence packet and approved structure.
## When to Use
Only when the researcher explicitly requests thesis prose.
## Required Inputs
Section map, verified claims/quotations, argument outline, style/citation requirements.
## Sources to Read First
Research Bible 03, 06–09, 15, 17, 19; section research packet.
## Procedure
State question and claim; develop connected paragraphs from verified evidence; distinguish author claims, interpretation, and researcher thesis; present objections/replies; add accurate citations; audit transitions and conclusion; update claims and bibliography.
## Evidence Rules
No placeholder passed off as citation. If evidence is missing, stop or visibly mark it.
## Output Format
Requested section prose plus concise evidence/audit note outside the thesis text.
## Quality Gates
Approved heading preserved; argument clear; all substantive claims supported; no generic filler; author voice preserved.
## Failure Conditions
Research packet incomplete, citation style unknown when final formatting is required, or P0 gap blocks argument.
## Prohibited Behaviors
Ghost-authorial certainty, fabricated citations, silent structural changes, inflated conclusions.
## Completion Criteria
Draft passes citation and argument audit and remains open to researcher revision.
