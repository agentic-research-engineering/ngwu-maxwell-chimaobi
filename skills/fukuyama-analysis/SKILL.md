# Fukuyama Analysis
## Purpose
Establish what Fukuyama argues before interpretation or criticism.
## When to Use
Any claim attributed to Fukuyama or comparison between the 2011 and 2014 volumes.
## Required Inputs
Question/concept, target section, canonical source copy.
## Sources to Read First
Research Bible 04, 06, 09, 11, 16; relevant full chapters, notes, and surrounding text.
## Procedure
Locate all relevant usages; read context; separate definition, illustration, causal claim, and conclusion; map premises/mechanism/qualification; compare later usages; record exact print/PDF location and conflicting passages; update claims/quotation ledgers.
## Evidence Rules
Use `[FUKUYAMA]` only for explicit or clearly developed claims. Mark reconstruction as `[INTERPRETATION]`.
## Output Format
Textual findings; argument reconstruction; qualifications/tensions; verified evidence entries; open issues.
## Quality Gates
Primary text controls; context preserved; volume II does not replace volume I; edition identified.
## Failure Conditions
Only derivative text available or pagination/context cannot be verified.
## Prohibited Behaviors
Quotation mining, harmonizing tensions silently, or attributing the project's synthesis to Fukuyama.
## Completion Criteria
Attribution and reconstruction are traceable to verified passages with uncertainty explicit.
