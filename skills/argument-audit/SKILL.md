# Argument Audit
## Purpose
Stress-test a section's reasoning before it is accepted.
## When to Use
After research synthesis or drafting and before chapter completion.
## Required Inputs
Argument/draft, claims register, supporting sources, section purpose.
## Sources to Read First
Research Bible 02, 04, 06, 07, 09, 17; cited evidence.
## Procedure
Extract claims and inferential links; classify each; locate support; test validity, hidden premises, equivocation, causal inference, counterexamples, contradictions, and scope; steelman objections; assign severity and repair.
## Evidence Rules
Every major premise needs an appropriate source or explicit researcher-argument defense.
## Output Format
Finding ID; claim/link; problem; severity; evidence; recommended repair; status.
## Quality Gates
Audit covers reasoning and evidence, not style alone; proposed repair preserves author intent.
## Failure Conditions
Sources/claim boundaries are unavailable.
## Prohibited Behaviors
Rewriting the thesis wholesale or treating disagreement as logical error.
## Completion Criteria
No unresolved high-severity flaw; remaining limits are disclosed.
