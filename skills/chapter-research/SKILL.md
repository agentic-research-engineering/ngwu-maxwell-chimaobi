# Chapter Research
## Purpose
Create a verified section research packet.
## When to Use
Before drafting any chapter section.
## Required Inputs
Exact TOC section, research-map row, section question.
## Sources to Read First
Research Bible 00–07, 09–10, 15–17, 19; relevant repository sources.
## Procedure
Confirm scope; review existing claims/gaps; list claims to establish; research primary then scholarly sources; extract/contextualize evidence; record objections and alternatives; update registers; prepare synthesis outline without thesis prose unless requested.
## Evidence Rules
Apply labels and source hierarchy; page-specific support for major claims.
## Output Format
Section; question/role; findings; claim-evidence table; objections; gaps; proposed argument sequence; updates made.
## Quality Gates
All research-map fields addressed; no unresolved P0 gap hidden; transitions/dependencies identified.
## Failure Conditions
Insufficient primary evidence or critical scholarship for central claims.
## Prohibited Behaviors
Changing TOC, duplicating research, drafting around missing evidence.
## Completion Criteria
The section can answer “what supports this sentence?” for every planned major claim.
